Fieldbook

A personal planner for PhD research and everyday tasks.

Fieldbook keeps tasks, routines and a research master plan (Gantt chart) in one place, with reminders on Android and Windows. It is a personal app built for one researcher's own use and is not offered as a public service.

What it does

  • Tasks, routines and a weekly timetable with reminders.
  • A master plan of work packages and milestones that can be exported to Excel.
  • Sync between the owner's own devices through the owner's own Firebase project.
  • Optional: attaching output files to tasks. Files are saved in the owner's own Google Drive, in a folder named "Fieldbook".

Google Drive access

If Google Drive is connected, Fieldbook asks only for the drive.file permission. It can see and change only the files it creates or uploads itself; it cannot see anything else in your Drive. See the privacy policy for details.

This site also hosts the update files the Fieldbook apps download.
