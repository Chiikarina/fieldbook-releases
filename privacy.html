Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Fieldbook is a personal planning app for Android and Windows. This policy explains what data it uses and where that data goes.

Data Fieldbook stores

  • Your plan: tasks, routines, the master plan and settings you enter.
  • Your sign-in: the email address and password you use to sign in. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; Fieldbook never sees your password in readable form.

This data is stored on your device and in the app owner's own Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) project, under your account. Security rules allow only your signed-in account to read or change it.

Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you connect it, Fieldbook requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission and your email address.

  • Fieldbook uses this access only to create a "Fieldbook" folder in your Drive, upload files you choose to attach, and open them for you.
  • Fieldbook can access only files and folders it created. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Fieldbook stores the name, size, type and link of each attached file in your plan so the file can be shown and opened from your tasks. The file contents stay in your Google Drive.
  • The permission token is stored in your plan's settings so all your signed-in devices can upload. It is never included in backup files.

Fieldbook's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Fieldbook does not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • Your data and Google user data are not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone else.
  • Google user data is not used for any purpose other than the features described above.

Removing access and data

  • Disconnect Google Drive in Fieldbook (Settings → Google Drive → Disconnect), or remove "Fieldbook" at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your files stay in your Drive until you delete them.
  • Deleting tasks or plan items in Fieldbook removes them from the synced copy.
